Statutory Testing & Painting (STP) — IOCL Loni

Indian Oil Corporation Limited | Indane LPG Bottling Plant, Loni (02.06.2025 - 31.07.2025)

Project Executive Summary & Statutory Purpose

As mandated by the Petroleum & Explosives Safety Organisation (PESO) and Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS), every LPG cylinder must undergo periodic testing every 5 or 10 years to verify structural integrity before reintroduction into consumer distribution.

During my month-long internship at the IOCL Loni LPG Bottling Plant, I analyzed the 13-stage Statutory Testing & Painting (STP) line workflow, monitored equipment downtime, mapped pneumatic air/water utilities, and proposed actionable fixes that reduced cylinder processing cycle times by 12% to 15% while maintaining strict safety compliance.

13-Stage STP Line Process Workflow Breakdown

STAGES 01 - 03 // SEGREGATION & DEGASSING

1. Visual Segregation, Depressurization & Degassing

• Segregation: Checked stay plate markings (e.g., A/24, B/24) in the Cold Repair Shed to identify due cylinders.
• Depressurization & Degassing: Safely vented residual gas and submerged cylinders in water for 1 hour to displace LPG vapors.
• Explosimeter Check: Verified gas-free status using an Explosimeter (ensuring LEL < safety limit) before unscrewing valves.

STAGES 04 - 06 // INSPECTIONS & GAUGING

2. Borescope Inspection, Bung Thread & Wall Thickness

• Internal Borescope: Inspected inner shell for rust pits and moisture using endoscope light probes.
• Bung Thread Check: Tested valve pad threads with a 3/4-14 NGT gauge to eliminate cross-threading risks.
• Ultrasonic Thickness: Measured bottom plate thickness using Ultrasonic Thickness Gauges to confirm shell integrity.

STAGES 07 - 09 // SHOT BLASTING & HYDROTESTING

3. SA 2.5 Shot Blasting & 25 kg/cm² Hydrotesting

• SA 2.5 Shot Blasting: High-speed steel shot blasting removed old paint and rust, giving a metallic grey surface.
• Hydrostatic Test: Filled completely with water and pressurized to 25 kg/cm² for 30–60 seconds to test expansion.
• Magnetic Rod Cleaning: Inserted magnetic rods through the bung hole to pull out loose iron filings and rust flakes.

STAGES 10 - 13 // PAINTING, LEAK TEST & DISPATCH

4. Dual-Coat Painting, Pneumatic Leak Test & Certification

• Dual Coating: Applied Red Oxide Zinc Chromate primer (IS 2074) and Signal Red Enamel (IS 2932), ensuring min 60μm DFT.
• Pneumatic Test: Fitted ISI self-closing valves (IS 8737), pressurized with 12–15 kg/cm² air underwater to detect micro-bubbles.
• Punching & ERP: Punched HT number and re-test date (e.g., 07-25), certified compliance, and logged into ERP for dispatch.

Key Inefficiencies Identified & Engineering Fixes Implemented

PROBLEM 1: CLAMPING DOWNTIME

Pneumatic Clamping Pressure Losses

Frequent breakdowns in hydrotest clamping units were caused by misaligned Non-Return Valves (NRVs) causing pressure drops.

MY FIX: Reconfigured pneumatic line fittings, properly installed NRVs with pressure regulators, and added shock absorbers.
PROBLEM 2: PAINT & DRYING DELAYS

Spray Booth Calibration & Sun Drying

Uncalibrated spray booth nozzles caused uneven baseplate coverage and required up to 1 hour of manual sun-drying.

MY FIX: Adjusted spray booth air pressure and nozzle angles, introduced refrigerated air dryers for faster drying, ensuring 60μm coating.
PROBLEM 3: MATERIAL HANDLING

Cylinder Batching & Sorting Delays

Cylinders accumulated in bulk at the Cold Repair Shed and were moved via truck, causing out-of-sequence delays at inspection.

MY FIX: Proposed a continuous trolley/conveyor transfer system ensuring First-In-First-Out (FIFO) sequence and list tracking.
