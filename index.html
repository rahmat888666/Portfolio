Case Study // Mechanism Design

Automatic Trash Can Lid Shutter

Problem Statement: Traditional touch bins or pedal bins present hygiene concerns on shop floors and public spaces. Standard motor hinges wear out fast due to large moment arms.

Engineering Action: Modeled a multi-blade circular iris aperture system in SOLIDWORKS. The design distributes mechanical stress along concentric linkage slots, reducing load on the servomotor.

Prototyping & DFM: Optimized design clearances (0.3mm stacking tolerance) suitable for FDM 3D printing. Configured blade hinges to prevent interlocking friction during high-speed openings.
